Getting here

You will find us at Dachauer Straße 91 in Maxvorstadt, between the central station and Stiglmaierplatz. If you arrive by train you can walk; from the airport or the trade fair it is one change.

How to reach us

From the central station

It is about a twelve-minute walk: leave the station by the north exit towards Arnulfstraße, head for Stiglmaierplatz and carry on along Dachauer Straße. With luggage, take the U1 or U7 one stop to Stiglmaierplatz – from there it is five minutes on foot.

From Munich Airport

The S1 and S8 trains both run into the city; the journey to the central station takes about 45 minutes. The airport coach also stops at the central station. From there, continue as above. By taxi you will be with us in 40 to 60 minutes, depending on traffic.

From Messe München in Riem

The U2 connects the exhibition grounds directly with the central station: no changes from Messestadt West or Messestadt Ost, around 25 minutes. After that it is a short walk, or one stop on the U1/U7 to Stiglmaierplatz. Coming back from the fair in the evening takes a good half hour door to door.

To the Allianz Arena

The U6 runs to the stadium at Fröttmaning. From us, take the U1 at Stiglmaierplatz to Sendlinger Tor and change to the U6 heading north – about 30 minutes in total. Trains are busy on match days, so allow a little extra time.

By car

Take the A9 or A8 towards the city centre, then follow the Mittlerer Ring and Dachauer Straße inbound. Please note Munich's low emission zone: you need a green sticker to drive inside the Mittlerer Ring.

Parking

We do not have our own garage. There are public car parks in the area, while street parking is mostly metered or reserved for residents. If you are driving, give us a call – we will tell you what works best at the moment.

The neighbourhood

  • MaxvorstadtThe university quarter, full of cafés, small restaurants and museums.
  • KunstarealThe Pinakothek museums, Lenbachhaus and Glyptothek are all within walking distance.
  • Augustiner-BräuThe traditional brewery is not far away.
  • Everyday thingsSupermarkets, pharmacies and snack bars are right around the corner.
