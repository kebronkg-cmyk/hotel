Frequently asked questions

When can I check in and out?

Check-in from 15:00, check-out until 11:00. Reception is staffed 07:00 – 22:00. If you will arrive later, please give us a quick call and we will sort it out.

Can I leave my luggage before check-in?

Yes. You can leave your bags with us before you check in and after you check out, and go and see the city in the meantime. Please keep valuables with you.

Is there parking?

We do not have our own garage. There are public car parks nearby, and most street parking is metered or reserved for residents. There is more on the Getting here page – or call us before you set off.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are welcome by arrangement [add the house rule and any fee]. Please tell us when booking so we can put you in a suitable room.

Can I pay cash?

Yes, you can pay cash with us. For the cards we also accept [add accepted cards], just ask us on the phone.

Is booking direct really cheaper?

Yes. Portals charge us a commission that ends up in the room rate. Book direct and it does not apply – so our own price is at least as good, usually better.
