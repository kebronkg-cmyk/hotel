Frequently asked questions

Is booking direct really cheaper?

Yes. Portals charge us a commission that ends up in the room rate. Book direct and it does not apply – so our own price is at least as good, usually better.

When can I check in and out?

Check-in from 15:00, check-out until 11:00. Reception is staffed 07:00 – 22:00. Arriving later is fine if you let us know – a quick call is enough.

Is there parking?

We do not have our own garage. There are public car parks nearby, and most street parking is metered or reserved for residents. You will find more on the Getting here page.

Can I cancel free of charge?

The cancellation terms are shown when you book and repeated in your confirmation. If your plans change, talk to us – with a direct booking we have the most room to help.

Is the hotel accessible?

This is an older building. Please call us beforehand and we will work out together which room suits you.
