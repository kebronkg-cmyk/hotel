Privacy policy

This website works without cookies, without tracking, without analytics tools and without external fonts or maps. We only process data that is technically needed to deliver the page, or that you send us yourself.

Note for the editor: please have this text reviewed legally before going live and name the hosting provider and the booking engine explicitly. The German version of this page is the legally binding one.

2. Visiting this website (server log files)

When you open these pages, our hosting provider automatically processes data that your browser transmits: IP address, date and time of the request, the file requested, the amount of data transferred, the browser used and the operating system. This processing is technically necessary to deliver the page and keep it secure. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in operating the site securely and without faults).

This website is hosted by [add hosting provider, e.g. GitHub Pages, GitHub Inc.]. The provider processes the access data described above on our behalf. Where data is processed outside the EU, this takes place on the basis of the safeguards provided by the European Commission.

3. Getting in touch

If you call us, send us an email or use the enquiry form, we process the details you provide (name, contact details, travel dates, the content of your message) in order to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to a contract, or performance of a contract) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

The enquiry form on this website does not send data to a server belonging to this site. It simply opens your own email programme with a prepared message, so it is sent through your own email provider.

4. Booking

If you book online, you are taken to the booking engine operated by [add booking engine provider], where that provider's privacy notice applies. We process the data collected there in order to perform the accommodation contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

5. Registration obligation for accommodation

As an accommodation business we are legally required to complete and keep registration forms on arrival (§§ 29 ff. of the German Federal Registration Act). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with that Act, which also sets the retention period.

6. Cookies, tracking and external services

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or marketing tools and embeds no external fonts, videos or social media elements.

Map (loaded on click)

On the „Getting here“ page the map is not loaded automatically. Only when you click „Load map from OpenStreetMap“ is the map section fetched from the servers of the OpenStreetMap Foundation. Your IP address and technical details of your browser are transmitted to OpenStreetMap in the process. The legal basis is your consent given by that click (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without the click, nothing leaves our site.

Map links and WhatsApp

Links to map services and – where set up – to WhatsApp only open the respective provider once you click them. From that point the provider's own privacy notice applies. If you write to us on WhatsApp, we process your message in order to answer your enquiry.

Enquiry form for groups and companies

By default, the form on the „Groups & companies“ page opens your own email programme. If a form service is set up instead ([add provider]), your details are transmitted to that service and forwarded to us from there. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

7. Retention period

We store personal data only for as long as it is needed for the respective purpose or as long as statutory retention periods require – in particular the six- and ten-year periods under German commercial and tax law.

8. Recipients

Your data is passed on only where this is necessary to perform the contract (for example to the hosting provider, the booking engine provider, payment service providers or our tax advisers) or where we are legally obliged to do so.

9. Your rights

You have the right to information (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16 GDPR), erasure (Art. 17 GDPR), restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR), data portability (Art. 20 GDPR) and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.

To do so, please contact info@hotel-montree.de.

10. Right to complain

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

11. Status

We update this privacy policy whenever the processing changes – for example once a booking engine is integrated.
