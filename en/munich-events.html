Oktoberfest, trade fairs and football

The three reasons most of our guests come to Munich – how to get there from the hotel, and when rooms start to run out.

The short version: if your trip falls in one of these periods, book early. Booking with us directly is not only simpler, it is usually cheaper than going through a portal.

Oktoberfest

The Wiesn runs for 16 to 18 days and traditionally ends on the first weekend in October. It opens with the tapping of the first barrel, and on the Sunday after that the costume and riflemen's parade moves through the city centre.

We are far enough from the noise for a quiet night and close enough to walk back in the evening. If you have a table reserved in one of the tents, book your room as early as you can – half the city is full at that time of year.

Getting there from the hotel

The Theresienwiese is a 20 to 25 minute walk away, or quicker on the underground from Stiglmaierplatz.

Messe München

The exhibition grounds in Riem host major trade fairs all year round, some annually, others every two or three years. On top of that there are events at the MOC and the Zenith in the north of the city.

For trade fair visitors the direct underground link is the practical part: no changes between the central station and the exhibition centre. We are happy to invoice your company, and for several rooms or regular stays there is our group and company enquiry.

Getting there from the hotel

The U2 runs from the central station to Messestadt West or Ost without changing, around 30 minutes in total.

Football at the Allianz Arena

On match days the city fills up from the afternoon onwards. If you are coming from out of town, staying near the station makes it easier: drop your bags, head to the stadium and come back afterwards without a long journey home.

The same connection works for concerts at the arena or in the Olympiapark.

Getting there from the hotel

Take the U1 from Stiglmaierplatz to Sendlinger Tor, then the U6 north to Fröttmaning – about 30 minutes altogether.

When rooms get scarce

  • OktoberfestThe busiest time of the year by far.
  • Major trade fairsIn fair weeks the whole city is booked up.
  • Home matches and concertsWeekends in particular.
  • AdventThe Christmas markets and Tollwood draw a lot of short-stay visitors.

Once your dates are fixed, it is worth securing the room straight away.
